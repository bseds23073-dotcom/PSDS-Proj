# Engagement journal (write this yourself, 1-2 pages of paragraphs; the headings below are the required order)

## Before I looked
Three predictions about your data, written and committed BEFORE loading it. Be honest about the commit time; if you had already looked at the data, say what you had and had not seen.

## What I did, in order
A short narrative including dead ends: what you tried first, what failed, what you changed.

## What surprised me
Anything that looked wrong or unexpected, and exactly how you checked whether it was real. (Things in the output you may want to investigate yourselves: the Sri Lanka mortality values in 2004 and 2009, the Pakistan unemployment values around 2012, and how the enrolment relationship changes when GDP and a year trend are added. Describe only what you actually checked.)

## What I revised
For each thing you changed your mind about (a five-question answer, a proposal assumption, a cleaning decision): what you thought before, what the data showed, and what you changed.

## What I still don't trust
At least two things about this data you would not yet bet a decision on.
