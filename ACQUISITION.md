The data comes from the World Bank's World Development Indicators (WDI), downloaded through DataBank as an Excel extract for Pakistan, India, Bangladesh, Nepal and Sri Lanka, 14 indicators, 2000-2023. The indicators are compiled by other agencies and republished by the World Bank: under-5 mortality by the UN Inter-agency Group for Child Mortality Estimation, female secondary enrolment by UNESCO's Institute for Statistics, fertility by the UN Population Division, health expenditure by WHO, and PPP GDP by the International Comparison Program, according to the metadata sheet. They were collected to monitor development goals (under-5 mortality is Sustainable Development Goal indicator 3.2.1) and to let countries be compared.

Could a stranger re-pull this next month? Mostly yes, but not with identical numbers. The metadata shows the series are revised (for example the enrolment data cites a UNESCO release dated 2026-02 and PPP GDP is in constant 2021 dollars), so a later download could shift values, add 2024 data and change which years are missing. The pipeline would break if DataBank changed the column names ("2000 [YR2000]") or the footer lines, because the loader keys on the "Series Code" column and the "[YR" year labels.
